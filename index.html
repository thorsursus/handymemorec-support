About the app

On Mac, Handy Meeting Notes records microphone and system audio with your permission, transcribes speech on-device with WhisperKit, and turns transcripts into structured meeting notes. You can search past meetings and ask the AI Coach questions about them. The default AI provider is Ollama at a local address; you can also configure a remote HTTPS Ollama endpoint with explicit confirmation or explicitly allow an optional OpenAI fallback using your own API key.

On iPhone and iPad, the companion app records microphone audio, transcribes it on-device, lets you edit or dictate notes, and searches your meetings. Text artifacts can sync with the Mac app through your iCloud account. Recording audio is never sent to an AI provider. See the Privacy Policy for iCloud and optional remote-provider details.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to run it?

The Mac app requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later. The iOS app requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Both apps transcribe on-device after downloading a speech model. The Mac's AI features (meeting notes, AI Coach, archive questions) need an AI provider: run Ollama with a downloaded model, configure another Ollama endpoint, or save an OpenAI API key as a fallback. Without an AI provider, recording and transcription still work.

Where are my recordings stored?

Mac recordings and their text artifacts are stored in the app's iCloud container, or in local Application Support if iCloud is unavailable. While retained, Mac source audio and any optional screen captures may sync through Apple's iCloud service. The iOS app stores its source audio locally and publishes meeting text into a shared iCloud container. After a successful transcription, source audio is removed according to the retention period you choose in Settings; failed recordings remain available for retry or deletion.

Does anything get sent to a server?

WhisperKit downloads the selected speech model from Hugging Face; transcription itself runs on your device. Apple's iCloud service may sync stored meeting data between your devices. The Mac app sends meeting text to the Ollama endpoint configured in Settings: the default address is local, but a custom HTTPS endpoint may be remote and requires your confirmation. If you save an OpenAI API key and Ollama is unavailable, the Mac app can send meeting text to OpenAI. The developer operates no collection server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I explore the app without recording a meeting?

Yes. On Mac, choose Open App Review Demo from the menu-bar menu. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → App Review Demo → Start Demo Mode. Each demo includes three fictional English meetings with transcripts, minutes, and notes. The sample library is separate from your own meetings and does not require microphone permission or iCloud. The Mac demo also includes prepared AI Coach and archive answers; generating new AI answers still requires a configured provider.

Recording starts but the transcription comes back empty or wrong.

Whisper needs clear speech and at least a few seconds of audio to work well. Very short clips or mostly silent recordings can produce inaccurate text or wrong language detection. If the language detection is consistently wrong, select a language in Mac Settings → Transcription Model or iOS Settings → Transcription → Language.

Meeting notes failed to generate. What now?

AI-generated notes run on Mac. Most often no AI provider was reachable. Check Mac Settings → AI Provider for live availability. If you use Ollama, make sure the configured endpoint is reachable and the selected model has been downloaded. Long meetings are processed in chunks and can take a while. Retry from the Transcripts window, or wait for the background retry.

Can I record other people without telling them?

You shouldn't, and in many places it's illegal. Recording-consent laws vary by country and state — in some jurisdictions all parties must agree. It is your responsibility to comply with local law and inform participants when recording.
