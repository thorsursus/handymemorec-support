Overview

Handy Meeting Notes ("the App") is a macOS menu-bar application that records meetings, transcribes them on-device with WhisperKit, and generates structured meeting notes with Apple Intelligence. The App is designed to be privacy-first: none of your audio, transcripts, or notes ever leave your Mac.

Data we collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. We do not operate any backend servers or cloud services. We have no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no way of knowing how you use the App.

What the App processes locally

To do its job, the App records and stores the following on your Mac only:

  • Microphone audio — captured only while you have explicitly enabled a recording from the menu bar. The macOS microphone permission prompt appears on first use.
  • System audio — captured via Core Audio's process tap, only while recording is active. The macOS audio-capture permission prompt appears on first use.
  • Transcripts — produced by Apple's WhisperKit speech-to-text framework, running entirely on your Mac.
  • Meeting notes — produced by Apple's Foundation Models framework (Apple Intelligence), running entirely on your Mac.

Source audio files are deleted automatically after a successful transcription. Only the resulting transcript and meeting notes are retained. If a transcription fails, the audio is kept until you retry or discard it.

Where data is stored

All recordings, transcripts, and meeting notes are stored in your iCloud Drive, inside the App's iCloud container (under iCloud Drive → Handy Meeting Notes → Recordings). If iCloud is unavailable, the App falls back to a local folder in macOS Application Support.

Because the data is in your iCloud Drive, it syncs across your devices via Apple's iCloud service. Apple's iCloud privacy policy applies to that sync; the App itself does not control or operate that channel.

Network use

The App makes one type of network request: WhisperKit downloads the speech-to-text model on first use from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is a one-time download of approximately 244 MB. After the model is on disk, the App works fully offline.

The App makes no other network requests. It does not contact our servers, third-party analytics, advertising networks, or AI services. Apple Intelligence runs entirely on-device on your Mac.

Apple Intelligence

The App uses Apple's Foundation Models framework for meeting-note generation, AI Coach observations, and archive search. Apple's framework runs the model locally on your Mac; no transcript or note content is sent to Apple's servers. The output is subject to Apple Intelligence's content guardrails, which may occasionally decline to process specific content.

Local storage

The App stores the following locally on your device:

  • Preferences (recording duration default, language, AI Coach configuration), in macOS UserDefaults.
  • Recordings, transcripts, and meeting notes, in the iCloud container (or local fallback).
  • The WhisperKit speech-to-text model, downloaded once.

Analytics and tracking

The App contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, or tracking of any kind.

Third-party services

Aside from the one-time WhisperKit model download from Hugging Face, the App does not communicate with any third-party service. Apple Intelligence is provided by macOS itself.

Recording-consent responsibility

Laws around recording conversations vary by country and state. In many jurisdictions, all participants must give consent. It is your responsibility as the user to comply with local law and to inform participants when you are recording.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
